Commands

Everything is typed in the channel

The commands are the ones OmenServe and iroffer users already know. The bot answers with a notice, so only the person who asked sees the reply. Here the bot is called alfa: use your bot's own nick.

Getting files
@alfaSends you the bot's list. The list goes ahead of queued files, so you never wait behind somebody's albums just to see what is there.
@find <words>Searches every bot in the channel at once. Each bot answers with its own matches.
@find "metal church" 1986Words in quotes must appear together, in that order.
!alfa <file>Asks for one file. Paste the line from the list or from a search reply. It also works as a private message to the bot, if you are in one of its channels.
!alfa !rar <folder>Asks for a whole folder, packed as one archive when your turn comes.
Your queue
@alfa-queShows what you are waiting for and where you are in line.
@alfa-removeCancels everything you have queued.
@alfa-remove <file>Cancels just that one file.
About the bot
@alfa-helpHow to use this bot, in a few short lines.
@alfa-statsWhat the bot has and what it has sent.
@alfa-topThe files and albums people ask for most.

For the operator: the admin console

The operator talks to the bot over a password-protected DCC CHAT. Type help there for the full list. These are the ones used most.

statusSlots, queue, speed and what is being sent right now.
queue [nick]Everyone waiting, in order, or one person's files.
queuemove <nick> up|downMoves somebody one place earlier or later in line.
clearqueue <nick>Clears somebody's queue.
ignore <nick> <minutes>Drops one person's requests for a while. It ends by itself.
ban <pattern>A permanent ban, such as *!*@10.0.0.?. unban removes it.
updateRebuilds the list from your folders.
listsThe other bots' lists you hold, and which of them have changed.
fetch [bot]Fetches the lists that changed, or one bot's list.
rehashReloads code and settings without dropping anybody's queue.
shutdown nowStops the bot.
