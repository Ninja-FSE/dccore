The bot as a window in mIRC

dccore.mrc connects your mIRC to your bot's admin console. You see every request, send and search as it happens, with the transfers live in a side panel, and you run the bot from menus and a control panel.

What it gives you

  • A live windowEvery event on its own line with a coloured tag. The window's button lights up on activity and turns red on a failed send.
  • A side panelEach running transfer with size, percentage and speed, who is waiting, and today's totals.
  • Menus for everythingRight-click a user in the panel or in any channel's nick list to see their queue, clear it or ignore them for a while.
  • A control panel Next releaseEvery bot setting in one window, saved with Apply. Your own mIRC's colours and switches beside them.
  • Downloads windowWhat the bot is fetching from other bots, with its queue.
  • DCCore ChatTalk to other DCCore operators in the channels your bot shares with theirs. It is public: everyone in the channel sees it.
  • Dark theme and icons Next releaseDark, light, or following mIRC's own dark mode.
  • Logs in by itselfYou type the password once when you pair. After that it reconnects on its own, and the password never touches the disk.

Install it

  1. Copy the script and its folders.

    Put dccore.mrc from the release's scripts/mirc/ anywhere, your mIRC folder is fine. Copy any folders beside it too. It needs mIRC 6.10 or later.

  2. Load it and pair it with your bot. 
    /load -rs dccore.mrc
/dccore pair alfa

    Use your bot's nick. The @DCCore window opens and the bot asks for its admin password. Type it in the window, once.

  3. Choose what you see.

    Type /dccore options for the colours, the side panel and the title bar. Right-click in the window for everything else.

Its commands
/dccore connectOpen the window and connect, logging in by itself.
/dccore disconnectClose the connection and stop reconnecting.
/dccore optionsWhat the window shows, its colours, the side panel and the title bar.
/dccore settingsNext release The control panel, on the bot's side.
/dccore chat [text]Open DCCore Chat, or say something in it.
/dccore downloadsWhat the bot is fetching from other bots.
/dccore theme darkNext release The script's windows dark. light and auto work too.
/dccore raw <command>Send any admin console command.
